PEB home designer

Frame, slab and wall selection, design loads, isolated footing, base plate and anchor checks for PEB homes (G+1 to G+4), using simplified IS 875, IS 1893, IS 800, IS 456 and ACI 318 methods. This is a decision-support tool. A qualified structural engineer must review and approve every result.

Grid and storey plans

Pick a storey and a tool, then click the plan. Columns toggle at grid points, walls on grid lines, openings (stair, lift) in cells, and Footprint marks cells that are outside that storey, so every storey can have its own shape, columns, walls and openings. Stair, Lift, Washroom and Balcony mark cells (they change loads and the BOQ). Window and Door mark existing wall segments. Mark stairs and lifts on every storey they serve. Perimeter walls start on. Grid values are in metres.
